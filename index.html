Project Context & Executive Summary

This project performs a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on an e-commerce customer behavior dataset. The raw data was programmatically synthesized to introduce missing records (5% age gaps), mixed date formats, and high-variance purchase outliers. We processed this dataset to build clean pipelines and uncover correlation factors driving spending and retention.

Missing Data Resolution: Imputed missing ages using median age calculation.

Outlier Strategy: Applied the IQR rule to cap extreme high spends, preventing skewed averages while retaining data rows.

Key Driver: Customer satisfaction rating represents the single largest correlative predictor of churn.

Understanding Statistical Relationships

We compute Pearson Correlation Coefficients ($r$) to evaluate strength and direction of linear associations:

Positive (0.5 to 1.0): Strong proportional relationship (e.g., Spend & Quantity)
Neutral (-0.2 to 0.2): No linear relation (e.g., Discounts & Spend)
Negative (-1.0 to -0.5): Inverse relationship (e.g., Satisfaction & Churn)

Key Structural Insights

  • Transactional Correlation: `Total_Spend_Cleaned` is highly correlated with `Items_Purchased` ($r = 0.90$).
  • Churn Correlation: Customer churn status shows a prominent negative correlation with `Average_Rating` ($r \approx -0.56$). Low satisfaction drives attrition.
  • Age Effect: Age shows a mild positive correlation with total spending ($r \approx 0.35$). Older consumers buy more.
